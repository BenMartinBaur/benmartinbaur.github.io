Careers rarely move in a straight line. The people who adapt well do not optimize for the next title alone; they build a portfolio that creates more ways to contribute.

## Invest in durable capabilities

Tools change quickly, but framing problems, communicating clearly, learning in public, and connecting technology to outcomes remain valuable. Treat these capabilities like compounding assets.

## Choose learning bets

A good learning bet sits at the intersection of curiosity, future relevance, and a real opportunity to practice. Pick one, create something visible, and use feedback to decide where to go deeper.

## Measure optionality

Progress is not only a promotion. It can be a new community, a stronger point of view, or the ability to solve a broader class of problems. These assets create options when the environment changes.
