Leadership in fast-moving environments is not the performance of having every answer. It is the practice of creating enough shared clarity for people to move with confidence.

## Name what is known

Separate facts from assumptions and open questions. This simple distinction reduces noise, makes disagreement productive, and prevents a temporary belief from becoming an invisible constraint.

## Make the next move small

When certainty is unavailable, lower the cost of learning. Define a reversible next step, the signal you expect to observe, and the moment when the team will decide again.

## Share the reasoning

People commit more deeply when they understand why a choice was made. Explain the trade-offs and invite better evidence. Clarity creates alignment without pretending the future is predictable.
