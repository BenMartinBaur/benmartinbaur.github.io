# Post 02 - Context Engineering Is the New Cost Engineering (Business)

## Hook
Your AI margin leak is usually context, not model pricing.

## Body
LLMs are stateless. Every turn re-sends context.
Unmanaged context becomes a recurring tax.

In most organizations, waste clusters in four places:
1. Oversized context windows
2. Stale conversation history
3. Over-generated outputs
4. Retry loops from vague instructions

The goal is not lower activity.
The goal is lower **cost per successful outcome**.
