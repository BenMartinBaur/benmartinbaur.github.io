# Post 00 - Token Economics in the Age of AI Agents (Series Intro)

## Hook
AI does not have a value problem.  
It has a management problem.

## Body
This topic is now front-and-center in enterprise conversations, and it has been highlighted by leaders across Microsoft, including Satya, Jerad, and Jörg Petter.

From what we see daily in the Microsoft Innovation Hub, the pattern is clear:
1. AI adoption is accelerating.
2. Agent usage is compounding.
3. Cost visibility is lagging behind execution.

FinOps is not new.  
What is new is the unit economics of agentic systems.

In classic IT, most cost lines were predictable.  
In AI systems, cost is usage-shaped and behavior-shaped:
- prompts and context size,
- model selection and routing,
- tool calls and retries,
- workflow depth and rework.

That is why this series focuses on one operating principle:
**optimize for cost per successful outcome, not cost per token alone.**

Over the next posts, I will break down practical patterns for:
- reducing token waste,
- improving attribution and governance,
- aligning architecture, product, and finance decisions.

## CTA
If you are building AI agents in production, what is your biggest blind spot today: **visibility, attribution, or control**?
