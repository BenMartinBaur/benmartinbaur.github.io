# Post 06 - Weekly AI FinOps Rhythm: Operate, Then Optimize (IT)

## Hook
Teams with stable AI economics run a governance rhythm, not one-off optimization sprints.

## Body
Use this weekly operating checklist:
1. Review spend by workflow, model, and team owner
2. Review run success rate and rework-token spikes
3. Tune context policy, routing thresholds, and output caps
4. Validate alerts, quotas, and budget policy thresholds
5. Capture top misses and add human-written instruction updates

Design your control surfaces by platform:
1. M365/Cowork: credit budgets, alerts, and policy limits
2. Copilot Studio: environment capacity and agent limits
3. Foundry/APIM: token limits, routing, telemetry, and governance policies
