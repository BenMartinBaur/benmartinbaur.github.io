# Post 07 - Agents are workforce, not middleware

## Hook
If agents participate in work, they need identity and accountability.

## Body
Agents execute work. They read documents, send emails, update records, approve requests.

In most organizations, they do this invisibly—through service accounts, shared credentials, or hidden API keys.

When something breaks, three questions surface immediately:
1. Which agent did this?
2. What permissions did it have?
3. Who owns it now?

Without answers, trust erodes.

The shift is simple but fundamental:
**Agents are not scripts. They are enterprise actors in your operating model.**

That means:
- Named identities (not "bot@company.com")
- Scoped privileges (not admin)
- Ownership assignments (not "IT manages it")
- Audit trails (not silent execution)

You would not hire a human with no name, unlimited access, and zero oversight.

## TL;DR
Agents that execute work need identity, least privilege, ownership, and audit trails—just like human workforce members.
