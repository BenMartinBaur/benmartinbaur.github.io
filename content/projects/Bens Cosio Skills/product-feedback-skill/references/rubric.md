# Rubric: Good vs Excellent Product Feedback

Score each required component on a 0-5 scale.

## What Help Is Needed
- **Good (3-4):** clear request type and blocked action.
- **Excellent (5):** precise expected behavior, trigger conditions, and implementation-ready scope.

## Customer Scenario & Desired Outcome
- **Good (3-4):** actor, task, and desired result are clear.
- **Excellent (5):** end-to-end flow, current-vs-desired behavior, and measurable success condition.

## Customer Impact
- **Good (3-4):** clear pain and consequence.
- **Excellent (5):** quantified impact (or high-confidence proxy), business + user impact, severity rationale.

## Workaround Details
- **Good (3-4):** workaround exists and has limitations.
- **Excellent (5):** step-by-step workaround, cost/effort, failure conditions, and why unsustainable.

## Overall quality label
- `insufficient`: missing required component or average < 2.5
- `good`: average 2.5-4.1 and all required components present
- `excellent`: average >= 4.2 and no component < 4

## Confidence
- `high`: strong direct evidence for key claims
- `medium`: minor inference required
- `low`: major claims weakly supported
